# Audio provenance
The kitten accent was recovered from the user's supplied Pounce-0.3.0.apk, resource raw/kitten_meows (res/_8.wav). The prior project's recorded upstream attribution was nathan-osman, CC0: https://freesound.org/people/nathan-osman/sounds/686775/

The new 16-second melody is an original procedural composition. The cat accent is blended at two phrase boundaries. The spoken prompt ("Take Pounce to your morning spot. Scan your code.") was synthesized locally with Windows Microsoft Zira Desktop and bundled for offline use. No speech service is invoked at alarm time.

Source reconstruction: scripts/make-audio.py and assets/audio/kitten_meows.wav. The supplied APK itself is not modified. Audio sample levels are checked for clipping; perceived loudness and intelligibility need checking on the user's phone.
